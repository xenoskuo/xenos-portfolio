AppFetch

專案背景AppFetch 是一款 Android App 整理工具，以申請專利中的技術自動分類手機中的 App。

核心概念依圖示顏色、字首字母與 Google Play 分類三種篩選條件，排成並列可捲動的分頁欄；點選任一分頁、再以另外一兩項交叉篩選，即可找到 App，不必離開主頁。

設計範疇包含 UI 流程、引導頁、對話框、色彩選擇、Banner 與 Logo／浮動視窗設計，以清爽的綠色系建立品牌識別。

BackgroundAppFetch is an Android app organizer that uses patent-pending technology to sort the apps on your phone automatically.

Core conceptApps are filtered by icon color, first letter and Google Play category, arranged as tabs on side-by-side scrollable columns. Tap any tab and narrow down with one or two of the others to find an app without ever leaving the main page.

Design scopeUI flow, onboarding, dialogs, color selection, banner and logo/floating-window design, with a clean green palette for the brand.
