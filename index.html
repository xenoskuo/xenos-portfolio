AppFetch

AppFetch 以申請專利中的技術，依圖示顏色、字首字母與 Google Play 分類自動整理 App，並排成可捲動的分頁欄；點選分頁再交叉篩選即可找到 App，不必離開主頁。

AppFetch's patent pending technology automatically organizes your apps by icon color, first letter of the alphabet and GooglePlay categories. These filters - icon color, first letter of the alphabet and GooglePlay categories - are then arranged on tabs on scroll able columns next to each other. You can then find your app by simply tapping anyone of the tabs and then narrowing down by one or two of the others. You never have to leave AppFetch main page to find your app.
